How these charts work

One sample per ten-minute UTC slot; each local date gets equal weight within an hour. Missing, invalid, and over-20-minute-old observations are excluded. Closed rental stations count as unavailable.

Highlighted hours need 3 distinct dates and 6 samples. The nearby group requires every member in the same snapshot. Repeated daylight-saving hours share a local date; skipped hours stay missing. Observations are not predictions or reservations.
